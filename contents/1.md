============================
    Hybrid Slice-by-Slice Architecture
=================================

So when we start from scratch, Slice 0 will establish the architectural direction and walking skeleton—not a complete architecture. Then Slice 1 will force us to make the first real domain, application, persistence, API, storefront, and dashboard decisions. From there, the architecture grows from actual evidence rather than speculation.

Our updated architectural principles

I would formally add these to our project rules:

Principle 1 — Vertical slices organize application capabilities

Each slice should deliver a complete business capability across the necessary layers.

Principle 2 — Domain models represent business knowledge

Business rules and invariants that transcend individual application flows belong in domain models.

Principle 3 — Don't abstract prematurely

Duplication isn't automatically bad during early exploration.

Extract when a concept is understood and genuinely shared.

Principle 4 — Cross-cutting concerns are centralized

Logging, authentication, authorization, tracing, recovery, rate limiting, etc. should be handled through middleware/pipelines/platform infrastructure where appropriate.

Principle 5 — Infrastructure supports the domain

Database, HTTP, queues, external services, etc. should not become the source of business rules.

Principle 6 — Architecture evolves through evidence

We establish direction upfront but allow implementation details to evolve as slices expose real requirements.

Principle 7 — Every slice must produce working software

A slice isn't complete because its code compiles. It must demonstrate the business capability end-to-end.
